# TaskDesk Privacy Policy

TaskDesk is a personal, open-source desktop application.

- Your tasks are stored locally on your computer (in a SQLite database under your Windows user profile) and in your own Google Tasks account via the Google Tasks API.
- TaskDesk connects only to Google's APIs (`tasks.googleapis.com`, `oauth2.googleapis.com`, `accounts.google.com`, `openidconnect.googleapis.com`) and only on your behalf, using OAuth credentials you authorize.
- Your Google sign-in token is stored in Windows Credential Manager on your machine. No data is sent to the app's author or to any third party.
- Nudge definitions and settings never leave your computer.
- To revoke access at any time, use the Disconnect button in TaskDesk's settings or remove TaskDesk's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: open an issue on this repository.
